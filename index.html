ABOUT THE PROJECT

About PowerGuard

PowerGuard is an educational electrical monitoring project that combines transformer load monitoring, electrical fault indication, and power factor improvement calculations in one dashboard.

Safety note: This website analyses entered values only. It does not connect to real electrical equipment, detect live faults, or automatically disconnect the power supply. Electrical work must be handled by qualified personnel.
